We are writing to ask whether our manuscript, “Domain-Gated Latent Diffusion for the Inverse Design of Novel HMX-Class Energetic Materials with First-Principles Validation,” would be a suitable contribution to the Special Issue “AI in Materials Design and Discovery” in Molecules, for which you serve as Guest Editor.

The paper applies a generative diffusion model to the design of new energetic molecules with targeted physical properties. Its central idea is to train the generator on data of mixed reliability while letting only the trustworthy measurements steer the design, so that weaker estimates do not push it toward implausible candidates; every generated molecule is then validated by quantum-chemistry calculations.

Abstract (excerpt). No new HMX-class energetic material has been reported in fifteen years, in part because reliable property data are scarce. We introduce Domain-Gated Latent Diffusion (DGLD), a generative diffusion model that designs new candidate molecules, letting only trustworthy data guide the design and validating each proposal with quantum-chemistry calculations. DGLD produced twelve new, computationally confirmed candidates in the HMX performance class, several of them chemically distinct from anything in the training set. Unlike the other generators we tested, it reliably proposes molecules that are both genuinely novel and on-target. The code and models are released openly.
